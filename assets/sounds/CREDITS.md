# Sound credits

One row per file in `sfx/` and `music/`: where it came from, who made it, and its license. Keep the link to the
license page as it was when you downloaded the file; it's the proof you may use it. A CC BY file needs its credit
line in the game's credits too. How the files get here: [docs/sound.md](../../docs/sound.md).
