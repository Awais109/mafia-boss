# 0039. Act II is gated by Act I goals, not Reputation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-15

## Context
Act I → Act II opened purely on `reputation >= 143` ([ADR 0015](0015-act-ii-pacing.md)'s numbers, tuned repeatedly in [TUNING.md](../../TUNING.md)). The 8 Act I goals ([ADR 0035](0035-guided-opening.md)) paid a gold bar each but gated nothing — a player could reach Act II having done none of them, including `actII` itself, one of the 8 goals, which just mirrored `state.act >= 2` back at the player as something to "complete." Goals needed to actually matter, and three of them needed to ask for more: owning two districts (not just controlling them, empty), working a front (not just toggling its dial once), and smuggling (not just once).

## Decision
- **The `actII` goal is removed.** It checked `state.act >= 2`, which is circular once Act II requires every goal done — it can never be the goal that finishes the list. Seven goals remain (`engine/config/schema.ts`'s `GoalId`, `engine/config/defaults.ts`'s `goals.list`).
- **Three goals redefined**, same ids, harder conditions (`engine/systems/goals.ts`'s `GOAL_CHECKS`):
  - `secondDistrict`: two districts controlled *and* fully built — every joint/racket slot the district allows, and every premises lot, filled. Reuses `openSpots`/`openLots` (`engine/systems/districts.ts`) rather than re-deriving slot logic.
  - `workFront`: both front types owned, each at rate level ≥ 2 (not capacity, and not just switching a mode once).
  - `smuggleRun`: 3 completed smuggling jobs, not 1.
  - `factoryTier2`, `thirdCrew`, `wardCop`, `soldier` keep their existing conditions — only their status changes, from optional to required.
- **The Act I → Act II transition moves from `reputation.ts` to `goals.ts`.** `checkActs` (`engine/systems/reputation.ts`) now only handles Act II being "cleared" (still `reputation >= actThresholds[3]`, unchanged — Reputation keeps doing everything else it always did: individual `unlockRep` gates, the 610 milestone, the general score). A new `checkActII` (`engine/systems/goals.ts`, exported for Debug's use) opens Act II the instant every id in `goals.list` is in `state.goals.done`, called once at the end of `checkGoals` — so several goals finishing on the same boundary only evaluate the transition once, and the transition depends only on the array that's actually changing, not on a Rep-changing action happening to coincide with the last goal.
- `reputation.actThresholds[2]` (143) is no longer read by any gate. Left in config, documented as historical: the unlock ladder (Restaurant at ★133, Act II racket types at ★148+) was tuned to sit around it, and that reference is still useful even though nothing checks it directly any more.
- **A new Debug action, `DEBUG_COMPLETE_GOALS`**, force-completes every goal (paying gold, same as the normal path) and calls `checkActII` directly — `checkGoals` itself waits for the tutorial to end, which a Debug/test shortcut shouldn't have to satisfy first. This replaces `DEBUG_SET_REP` as the fast path to Act II for Debug and tests.
- **The sim bot's persona gained deliberate goal-pursuit logic** (`sim/persona.ts`), because its normal economic-optimization logic doesn't reliably trigger `factoryTier2`, `smuggleRun`, or `workFront` — each only fires on a predicted shortage or high front utilization, both rare under the tuned economy (confirmed against `TUNING.md`'s own logged history: shortages are "rare... none in Act I," front utilization has never logged above ~0.68). A real player would just do these deliberately once they know goals gate Act II, so the bot does too: it runs right after `COLLECT`, ahead of the bot's usual spend/dispatch priority, gated on the same wage/upkeep reserve health the rest of the bot respects (a new business or upgrade adds ongoing upkeep, and the district-completion logic additionally caps new premises upkeep at the same wage-share ceiling used for hiring, so pursuing a goal is never a reason to run upkeep or crew dispatch into the ground). It runs *before* the deposit step specifically, so a session's deposit reserve already reflects any new upkeep the goal-pursuit adds that same session — moving it after deposits (the first attempt) left a one-session gap that showed up as real missed paydays in the sim.
- Bumped `SCHEMA_VERSION` 7→8: an old save's `state.goals.done` can contain `'actII'`, no longer a valid `GoalId`. `v7to8` (`engine/model/migrate.ts`) filters it out; `state.act` itself is untouched either way (a save already in Act II under the old Rep gate stays there).

## Consequences
- Act I pacing changes substantially: casual-bot mean clear time moved from ~1.9 days (Rep-gated) to ~4.5-5.5 days (goal-gated) — a direct, expected cost of requiring real deliverables instead of a Reputation number. `tests/sim.test.ts`'s guards were updated to the new measured range, not loosened past what the bot actually does.
- Act II now tends to clear faster once reached (~1-2.2 days after Act I, down from 3-5) — a bot that took this long to finish Act I's goals enters Act II already heavily built up.
- Gold bars barely accelerate Act I any more, since gold speeds up job completion and the new gate is mostly building-driven, not job-driven. The `leaves Act I at a day or more` guard in `tests/sim.test.ts` still passes, just with much more margin than before.
- The Home and Header Act I progress displays (`app/screens/HomeScreen.tsx`, `app/components/Header.tsx`) now show goals completed, not a Rep fraction, while in Act I; Act II's own Rep-fraction-to-cleared display is unchanged.
