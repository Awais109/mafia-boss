# 0042. Act III's consequences: contests, injuries, Tolya's attacks, loans and the loan desk

- **Status:** Accepted; superseded in part by [0051](0051-rock-bottom.md) (a second missed loan payment repossesses a business instead of taking a share of the vault)
- **Date:** 2026-09-29

## Context
The second half of the Act III design (expansion plan §14): borrowing against income with collectors if a payment is missed; a loan desk that lends money out and sometimes loses it; injuries from failed rough work and a Clinic to shorten them; and hostile rivals attacking businesses, answered by paying, fighting or hunkering down. Several of these needed a chance-based choice inside a decision ("send someone out"), which the inbox couldn't express: an option's effects were fixed numbers, materialized when the item was filed.

## Decision
- **Contests in the inbox.** An option may carry `contest { stat, diff, win, lose }`. When it's chosen, the best available crew member for the stat (idle first, then an enforcer) rolls `stat + U(−ops.noise, ops.noise)` against `diff` on `rng.derive('contest', itemId)`: one roll per item, fixed by its id, so timing can't reroll it. The branch it lands on applies like any option's effects. `contestOdds` is the closed form, shown on the card and used by the bot. A default can't be a contest, and a contest can't nest another.
- **Materialized context.** Effects are still fixed at filing, now against what the item is about: `dirtyPerDue`/`cleanPerDue` scale by an amount (a missed payment, a defaulted loan), `stashConditionMult` softens damage when a Stash House shares the business's street, and a contest's `enforcerBonus` lowers its difficulty when the business has an enforcer. Incidents a system files (`filed: true`) never come from the random roll.
- **Injuries**, from Act III (`injuries.*`): a failed job whose weights lean at least `minMuscleWeight` on Muscle hurts one of its team with `chanceOnFail` (on `rng.derive('injury', opId)`), and a lost contest can hurt the fighter (`injureHours`). A hurt member (`status: 'injured'`, `injuredUntil`, a reconcile boundary) can't work or be arrested, still draws wages, and comes back on their own. **The Clinic** (premises, one per city) scales injury time by `injuryMult` and gives everyone `loyaltyPerDay` at each day start. The plan's "pay a doctor" option without a Clinic was dropped: the Clinic is the answer, and a second path added a decision with no interesting side.
- **Tolya's attacks.** From `rivals.tolya.attack.fromAct`, each of his visits may also send his boys against a joint or racket: `chance`, `chanceNoTurf` once you've taken his district, `chanceHostile` when he's hostile. It's an incident (`attack`): board it up (the default: condition loss, halved beside a Stash House), pay an hour of the city's takings, or fight (Muscle; win: Rep and a little more of his ill will; lose: damage and an injury). The plan applied attacks to any hostile rival; only Tolya visits on a schedule, so only he attacks. Zhanna's leverage stays in her prices.
- **Loans** (`credit.*`). One at a time, up to `maxDaysOfClean` days of the mean daily Clean on the ledger's closed days (at least `minCap`). Borrowing adds Clean with no Rep. At each day start after upkeep, interest accrues on what's owed and `repayPctPerDay` of the principal plus that interest is due **from Clean only**: repaying in Dirty would have been a better laundering rate than any front. A payment Clean can't cover is missed and files the collectors (`collectors`: let them make a point, pay double in Clean, or show them out); a second miss takes `secondMissVaultPct` of the vault. `REPAY_LOAN` pays down early.
- **The loan desk** (premises, one per city): `LEND` puts up to `lendHoursPerTier × tier` hours of Dirty yield out for `lending.termHours`. When it's due (a reconcile boundary) it comes back with `returnPct`, unless the borrower defaults with `max(minDefault, defaultBase − defaultPerProsperity × the desk's street)`, on `rng.derive('lend', id)`: then a `lendingDefault` incident lets you write it off or send someone after half of it. Dirty in, Dirty out: it's a return on idle cash, not a way to launder.

## Consequences
- The bot wins most fights (its crew are strong by Act III), borrows now and then for a purchase that pays for itself inside two days, and lends heavily once it has a desk. None of it moves Act III's length (6.4 days) or heat (33.3).
- Attacks come about once a day in a run where the bot has taken Kiosk Row, more when Tolya's hostile; winning fights sours him, so a player who always fights sees more of them.
- Dirty left idle at session end doesn't improve: the loan desk takes one loan at a time, and late Act III yield outruns it and the fronts together.
- Save schema v10.
