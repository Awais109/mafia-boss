# Sevgorod: privacy policy

*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

Sevgorod is a game you play on your phone. This is what it does with information about you: almost nothing.

## What stays on your phone

The game keeps three files on your phone, in its own storage:
- **your save:** your empire, your crew, your progress;
- **your settings:** sound, music and notifications;
- **a play log:** what you did in the game and when, and any error the game ran into.

They're never sent anywhere by the game. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you send it. The game has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

If you choose **Share my play log** (in Settings, or on the "Something went wrong" screen), your phone's share sheet opens, and you decide where the file goes: an email to us, a message, or nowhere. Test builds can also export the save itself, the same way. Whoever you send it to receives it. If you send it to us, we use it only to understand how the game played for you and to fix what went wrong, and we delete it when we're done.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your phone: the vault is full, a decision is waiting, today's gold. They're set up on the phone itself. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or your phone's settings.

## Children

Sevgorod is a game about crime in a 1990s city and isn't meant for children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If the game ever adds an account, cloud saves, analytics, ads or purchases, this policy will say so, in plain words, before that version is released.
